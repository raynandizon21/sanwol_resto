# Attendance and employees

How the app turns MB160 punches into attendance (hours worked per day), and how employees are managed. The device side (ADMS protocol, device users, punches, corrections) is in [zkteco-mb160.md](zkteco-mb160.md).

## Status (2026-11-06)

Punches are paired into time-in → time-out shifts and added up per day. Since 2026-11-06, **schedules are set per employee per day** on the **Schedules** page (a month or week grid, like a posted roster): each day is a shift (one of Sanwol's fixed shifts, e.g. *Opening 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM*; Mid and Whole day on Fri–Sun only), a day off, leave, a recorded absence, or blank (not scheduled). Late, undertime and absences are checked against each day's shift, and regular hours go up to the shift's length. A blank day isn't checked (hours still count). The 2026-11-05 version (a branch schedule followed by everyone unless their profile had their own or "No schedule", plus per-person day marks) was replaced; it held no data. **No attendance settings** (since 2026-11-06, at the senior's request; Sanwol sorts out what's logged): the per-branch rules (standard day, longest shift, grace period, cutoff) and branch holidays were removed, and the cutoff is fixed at the 1st–15th and the 16th–end. User IDs are assigned behind the scenes and not shown (except to tell apart punches of someone not on the device). Overtime and payroll are not built (payroll is on hold); what was planned is in *Not built yet* at the end.

## Schedules (`/schedules`)

A grid like a posted roster: one row per person on the branch's device, grouped by position (in the Position dropdown's order, names A–Z inside), one column per day. **Month** (default), **Cutoff** (the 1st–15th or the 16th–end, since 2026-11-09) or **Week** (Monday–Sunday), with ‹ ›. The grid has no vertical scrollbar of its own (since 2026-11-09): its rows share the window's height (`GRID_CHROME_PX` in `Schedules.tsx` is what's reserved above and below it, `ROW_MAX_PX` caps them for a short filtered list), so Sanwol's 22 people fit on one screen on a 1080p monitor. If a window is too short for everyone, the rows stop shrinking and the page scrolls instead. Weekends are shaded and today's date is highlighted (the *Today* button was removed 2026-11-06). People removed from the device are listed (greyed) only when they have days in the range; days before the hire date or after the last day of work are hatched (a shift there isn't expected). A footer row counts the people on shift per day.

- **Setting days**: click a day, or **drag a rectangle** across days and people, then pick a **shift** (one click), **Day off**, **Leave** or **Absent** (both with an optional note), or **Clear** (not scheduled). One request per selection (`PUT /attendance/schedule`). A shift that isn't for every selected weekday (e.g. a drag from Thursday to Saturday) isn't listed (hidden, not greyed out, since 2026-11-06; if none fits, the dialog says so), so whatever is picked lands on every selected day; off duty and Clear always work. The server refuses a shift on a weekday it isn't for. *Custom times* were removed 2026-11-06. Keyboard: Enter or Space on a focused day opens it. Escape cancels a drag.
- **Shifts are fixed** (since 2026-11-06, senior's request; the *Shift types* button and editing were removed), from Sanwol's FOH schedule, in this order: **Opening 10:00 AM – 7:00 PM** (every day), **Mid 12:00 PM – 9:00 PM** (Fri–Sun), **Closing 2:00 PM – 11:00 PM** (every day), **Whole day 11:00 AM – 11:00 PM** (Fri–Sun; the grill attendants' 11AM–11PM). So Mon–Thu have Opening and Closing, Fri–Sun all four. They're defined in `ScheduleModel.FIXED_SHIFTS` and written into every branch's `attendance_shift_types` rows on start (`syncFixedShifts`; a branch gets them on first use); changing a shift is a code change. Each day keeps a copy of its times. On start, days the rule doesn't allow (a Mid / Whole day on Mon–Thu, a custom-time shift) are deleted (`clearDisallowedDays`; 6 test days were cleared on 2026-11-06). The page's legend is a **Shift | Time in | Time out** table like the printed sheet's (since 2026-11-09; the days are no longer listed there, the set-shift dialog only offers what each day allows).
- **Position filter**, **Print** and **Download**. Each makes the sheet for **one cutoff** (1st–15th or 16th–end, since 2026-11-06): both list the cutoffs the view overlaps (two in Month view, one in Cutoff view, one or two in Week view; *Current* marks today's) and act directly when there's only one. **Download** saves the PDF (separate button since 2026-11-06). **Print** goes **straight to the browser's print screen** (`printPdf.ts`, since 2026-11-06: the PDF is printed from a hidden frame, no download or in-app preview; the print screen's *Save as PDF* saves it). It loads that cutoff's days itself (so a week view can print the whole half-month), with the position filter applied. The sheet (A4 landscape, `schedulePdf.ts`): the branch, the period (*November 1 - 15, 2026*) and the position filter at the top; people grouped under position headings (like the page) with No. and name; one column per day showing only the day number and weekday (weekends in a lighter heading, blank weekend days shaded); shift names in their colors at one text size chosen so no name ever breaks mid-word (a two-word name may wrap at the space); an *On shift* count per day; a legend table like the posted schedule's (**Shift | Time in | Time out** only; the Days column and the off-duty table were removed 2026-11-06); **one page when it fits** (since 2026-11-06 the sheet is built in the roomiest of three row densities, `LAYOUTS` in `schedulePdf.ts`, that puts the grid, *On shift* row and shift table on one page; Sanwol's 22 people fit with either cutoff length; a staff too big for any of them prints in the roomy layout across pages); *Printed …* and *Page x of y* on every page, with the heading and day columns repeated. The page reloads by itself when anything changes (no Live badge or Refresh button since 2026-11-06).
- **Removed 2026-11-06**: *Today*, *Live* / *Refresh*, *Copy last week* (and `POST /attendance/schedule/copy-week`) and *Attendance rules* (hours, cutoff, holidays).
- Colors: shift types use their own color (text black or white, whichever reads better); Day off gray, Leave violet, Absent red (the same as the day statuses in Attendance Logs).

## Profiles (`sanwol_resto.employee`)

- **One `employee` row per person, and it is also their device user** (since 2026-11-05): the profile columns below plus the device's `ZK_` columns (`ZK_PIN`, `ZK_NAME`, `ZK_ON_DEVICE`, …; see *Database* in [zkteco-mb160.md](zkteco-mb160.md)). Someone created on the device gets a row the moment the device reports them (named as on the device), in the same transaction (`admsService.saveUser`). Removing someone from the device keeps their row (`ZK_ON_DEVICE = 0`).
- **Add employee** sends the profile details with the device command; the row is created only once the device confirms the new user (see *Adding users from the app* in [zkteco-mb160.md](zkteco-mb160.md)). **Add again** puts the same row back on the device and updates its details.
- Someone deleted and re-created *on the device* gets a new row; **Profile → Returning employee?** (`POST /attendance/employees/link`, `ZktecoModel.mergeIntoFormerEmployee`) moves their device link, punches and day marks onto their old profile, and the new row is deactivated (`ACTIVE = 0`).
- **The name on the device follows the profile name** (`services/deviceNameSync.js`): saving a profile queues the profile name, shortened to the device's 24 characters, whenever it differs from what the device has (or will have after a pending rename). Role, card and password stay as the device has them. A pending removal skips it. A rename made on the device itself is not copied back: the profile is the source of truth, and the list shows *On device: …* when they differ. Shortening (`deviceNameFor`, server `utils/employeeProfile.js`, mirrored in `src/components/attendance/profileFields.tsx`): *First Last* → *First L.* → fewer first-name words with the initial → fewer first-name words → first word cut to 24 characters.
- Columns used: `FIRSTNAME`, `LASTNAME`, `DEPARTMENT` (shown as *Position*), `DATE_STARTED`, `DATE_SEPARATED`, `CONTACTNo`, `BIRTHDATE`, `ADDRESS`, `EMERGENCY_CONTACT_NAME/PHONE`, `NOTES`, `EXCLUDE_FROM_ATTENDANCE` (0/1; API field `exclude_from_attendance`; briefly named `NOT_STAFF`, renamed or dropped by `EmployeeModel.ensureSchema`). `USER_INFO_ID` and `BRANCH_ID` are optional (kitchen staff have no login). No schedule columns: `EmployeeModel.ensureSchema` drops the first version's `EMPLOYMENT_TYPE`, `SHIFT_START`, `SHIFT_END`, `BREAK_MINUTES`, `REST_DAYS` (2026-11-04) and the 2026-11-05 `SCHEDULE_MODE`, `SCHEDULE_START`, `SCHEDULE_END`, `SCHEDULE_REST_DAYS` (2026-11-06; schedules are in `attendance_schedule_days`). The legacy `/employees_list` and `/employee/:id` API is untouched (it lists explicit columns, so it never returns the `ZK_` ones).
- The Profile tab and Add employee share their fields and checks (`profileFields.tsx`; server `utils/employeeProfile.js`).

## The attendance engine (`server/services/attendanceEngine.js`)

Pure function (no DB, no settings): one person's punches (+ their schedule per day) → one record per day. Unit tests: `cd server && npm test` (`attendanceSchedule.test.js` covers late, undertime, absences, days off, leave, recorded absences and regular hours against the shift). `services/attendanceService.js` loads the data (punches from `zk_attendance_logs`, people from `employee`, schedule days from `attendance_schedule_days`) and runs it per person.

**Input punches** are every punch in `zk_attendance_logs`, device and manual, at its corrected time and status, deleted ones left out (`ZktecoModel.getPunchesForAttendance`). Each carries its `EMPLOYEE_ID`, set when it arrived. A day before and after the range are loaded so shifts crossing the edges are complete.

**Shifts.**
- Every punch is either **arriving** (Time-in 0, Break-in 3, OT-in 4) or **leaving** (Time-out 1, Break-out 2, OT-out 5). Whatever key staff press, time between arriving and leaving is time at work. Older punches with no state (255) alternate in / out and are flagged *inferred*.
- A time-in opens a shift; the next time-out closes it, however much later (there's no *Longest shift* setting since 2026-11-06; one over 16 h needs fixing, see *Problems*). A time-out with no open shift is a shift with **no time-in**; a second time-in while one is open leaves the first **without a time-out**.
- The same kind of punch again within 5 minutes is a double tap: the first time-in counts, and the last time-out.
- **A shift belongs to the day of its time-in**, so 6 PM – 3 AM is one day.
- A time-in with no time-out yet is **on shift** (not a problem) for up to 16 h; after that it's missing its time-out.

**Hours per day.**
- **Worked** = the sum of the day's complete shifts (time-in → time-out). No break is deducted: time clocked out (e.g. for lunch) simply isn't counted. Each shift is measured to the second and rounded **down** to whole minutes (08:00:59 → 17:00:00 is 8h 59m).
- **Regular** = worked, up to the **day's shift length** when they have a shift (9 h for Opening, 12 h for Whole day); a day without a shift is **all regular** (the *Standard day* setting was removed 2026-11-06). **Over** (*Over the shift*) = the rest. Nothing is lost; "over" is where overtime rules can start later. No break is deducted (decided 2026-11-06: a 9-hour shift is 9 regular hours).

**Schedules, late, undertime, absences** (from the Schedules page; `computeAttendance({ roster })`, a map of date → entry).
- A day is a **shift** (start and end, from one of the fixed shifts), **off duty** (*Day off*, *Leave*, *Absent*, with an optional note) or **not scheduled** (no entry). A shift only counts between the hire date and the last day of work (when set). There's no grace period (removed 2026-11-06).
- **Late** = the day's first time-in after the shift's start, in whole minutes, counted **from the start** (10:00 start: in at 10:00:59 → not late; at 10:01 → 1 min; at 10:20 → 20 min).
- **Undertime** = the day's last time-out before the shift's end, on a complete day (not one to fix or still running). An end at or before the start is the next day's (18:00 – 02:00).
- **Absent** = a past day with a shift and **no punches at all** (a day with a broken punch is *Needs fixing* instead; today and later days are *Upcoming*), or a day recorded **Absent** (shown ahead of time too). If someone recorded absent punches in anyway, **the punches win**: the day is worked, with the note *Marked absent on the schedule, but punched in* (flag `marked_absent`).
- **Day off** (status `rest_day`) and **Leave** (status `excused`) are never absences, shown ahead of time too. Coming in on one, or on an unscheduled day, counts the hours, never late or undertime.

**Problems** (day status *Needs fixing*; the broken shift isn't counted until fixed): `missing_in`, `missing_out`, `long_shift` (a shift over 16 h: almost always a forgotten time-out joined to a later one). Note: `inferred`. Fix them with **Add punch** / **Edit punch** in the timesheet. Add punch warns when a time-out is entered before the shift's time-in (a night shift's time-out after midnight goes on the next date).

**Day statuses**: `worked`, `incomplete` (needs fixing), `on_shift`, `off` (no punches, not scheduled), `upcoming` (later, or today with a shift and no punch yet), `absent`, `rest_day` (day off), `excused` (leave). (`holiday` was removed with branch holidays.) Each day also has `scheduled` (had a shift), `shift` (`{ shiftTypeId, name, color, start, end }`), `offDuty` (`{ type, note }`), `lateMinutes` and `undertimeMinutes`.

**Totals** per person: days worked (days with a time-in), hours worked, regular, over, needs fixing, absent days, late days and minutes, undertime days and minutes, days on leave and days off actually taken (`excusedDays`, `dayOffDays`; coming in anyway is a worked day, not counted twice), days with a shift (`scheduledDays`; the pages use it to tell whether anyone is scheduled), and the average per day (over days with counted hours).

## Removed settings (2026-11-06)

The per-branch **Attendance rules** (`attendance_settings`: standard day, longest shift, grace period, cutoff type and days) and **branch holidays** (`attendance_holidays`) were removed at the senior's request; both tables held only test data and are dropped on start (`ScheduleModel.dropRemovedTables`). The engine now needs no settings (see above). The cutoff is fixed at the 1st–15th and the 16th–end (`timesheetPeriods.ts`).

## Schedule tables

**Shift types** (`attendance_shift_types`: `BRANCH_ID`, `NAME`, `START_TIME`, `END_TIME`, `COLOR` (hex), `WEEKDAYS` (allowed weekdays, `0,1,…` with 0 = Sunday), `SORT_ORDER`, `ACTIVE`; all kept in line with `FIXED_SHIFTS`) and **schedule days** (`attendance_schedule_days`: `EMPLOYEE_ID` (FK `employee`, cascade), `WORK_DATE`, `DAY_TYPE` = `shift` / `day_off` / `leave` / `absent`, `SHIFT_TYPE_ID` (FK, NULL = off duty; SET NULL if a type row is ever deleted), `START_TIME` / `END_TIME` (the shift's copy), `NOTE`; unique per person and date; clearing a day deletes its row) are in `models/scheduleModel.js`. The 2026-11-05 day marks (`attendance_excused_days`) are moved into schedule days on first start (leave → leave, day off → day off, holiday → leave noted "Holiday") and the table is dropped; it was empty. "Returning employee?" moves schedule days to the old profile with the punches.

Changing a schedule day recomputes everything on the fly (attendance is never stored).

## Manual punches and punch types (`zk_attendance_logs`, `SOURCE = 'manual'`)

For a forgotten punch: **Attendance Logs → Punches → Add punch**, or **Add punch** on a timesheet day (it suggests the missing type). A reason is required. They're rows of `zk_attendance_logs` with `SOURCE = 'manual'`, no `DEVICE_ID`/`PIN`, and the person's `EMPLOYEE_ID` (so PIN reuse can't move them). They show in the punch list as *Added manually* (verified by "Entered in the app"), count like device punches, and are edited, deleted (soft) and restored through the same endpoints as device punches. A manual punch can't share the exact time of another of that person's counted punches.

**A reason is required** to add, edit or delete any punch (device or manual). Only setting a device punch back to the device's record and restoring a deleted punch need none.

The app sets only **Time-in / Time-out** (adding, and editing any punch). Editing can also keep the type a punch already has (e.g. an OT-in recorded by the device), so an edit never forces a change of type. The server enforces the same.

*Device setting to consider:* the MB160's punch-state keys can usually be limited to Time-in and Time-out (on the device, around Personalize → Shortcut Keys; check the exact menu). Not required: the other keys count as arriving or leaving anyway.
